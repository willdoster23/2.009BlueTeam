Driver feedback · optional wiring

With power off: ALM+ → D2, ALM− → GND; PEND+ → D3, PEND− → GND. These isolated outputs use the Nano's internal 5 V pull-ups. Do not connect an external 24 V pull-up to these Nano inputs.

Building size

Enter the approximate size of the wall to scan. The map draws it as a rectangle and splits it into square photo frames.

Default 0.61 m = 3 standard bricks across (3 × 8 in, mortar included). After each vertical strip the scanner moves right by exactly this width, so photos don't overlap. Set it to what your camera actually covers (e.g. 0.7 m).
